Inclusive design

Inclusive design ensures that every person—regardless of their gender, location, native language and physical abilities—can enjoy and use products or services.

As Human Centered Designers we often focus heavily on the accessibility of our designs. While important, being accessible alone is not enough to ensure our work is truly human centered. Also think about inclusivity. Yes, your product might be accessible - but is the content mindful of the types of people using the service? HCD should be accessible and inclusive.
