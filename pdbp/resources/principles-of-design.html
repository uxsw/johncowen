Principles of design

Steve Jobs famously said: “The design is not just what it looks like and feels like. The design is how it works.”This is true. However a beautifully designed interface can increase user engagement by offering them a more delightful experience. A good understanding of the principles of design will help you deliver highly functional and accessible solutions that also have a strong design aesthetic.

The seven principles of design

Classic and digital designers alike harness the principles of design to shape their work, building on top of the Elements of design to give cohesion to their pieces. When used successfully, these principles have the ability to transform designs, both aesthetically and in terms of performance.
